Flatout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Flatout is an independent, unofficial Formula 1 companion app. This policy explains what information the app does — and does not — handle. It is written to be short and honest.

No account, no personal data collected by us

Flatout does not require you to create an account and does not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email, or contacts. Your in-app preferences — spoiler-free mode, appearance (light/dark), temperature units, and your Flatout Pro status — are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free tier of Flatout displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device and advertising identifiers and related data. On iOS you will be asked (App Tracking Transparency) whether to allow tracking, and you can decline. Flatout Pro removes all ads. See Google's privacy information:

Google Privacy Policy · AdMob & data

Purchases (Apple & RevenueCat)

Flatout Pro is sold as an in-app purchase. Payments are handled by Apple; Flatout never sees your card or payment details. To manage your subscription entitlement, purchase receipts and an anonymous app-user ID are processed by RevenueCat. See:

RevenueCat Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy

Data sources

Flatout displays public information fetched directly from third-party services: race data from Jolpica/Ergast and OpenF1, weather from Open-Meteo, and news headlines from public RSS feeds. When the app requests this data, standard network requests (including your device's IP address) are made to those services under their own policies.

We do not sell your data

Flatout does not sell your personal information to third parties.

Children

Flatout is a general-audience sports app and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
