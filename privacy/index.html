FlatOut — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

FlatOut is an independent, unofficial Formula 1 companion app published by Neo Media Productions Inc. (“we,” “us”). We built FlatOut to be private by default. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

  • FlatOut has no user accounts and no login. We don’t ask for your name, email, or any personal details.
  • FlatOut has no servers or database of our own. Your settings live only on your device.
  • We do not collect analytics, and version 1 of the app shows no ads and performs no advertising tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a few preferences locally on your device (using the operating system’s standard on-device storage) so it can remember your choices between launches: your spoiler-free setting, temperature unit, time-zone/city selection, and whether you have an active FlatOut Pro subscription. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Information sent to third parties

To show live standings, results, schedules, weather, and news, the app requests data from third-party services. These requests are made directly from your device:

  • Jolpica F1 API and OpenF1 — Formula 1 schedule, results, standings, and statistics.
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts for circuit locations.
  • News publishers (The Race, RaceFans, Autosport, Motorsport.com, Formula1.com) — headlines are fetched from their public feeds; tapping an article opens it on the publisher’s own site.

As with any internet request, these providers may automatically log technical information such as your IP address and device/browser type in their own server logs. We do not control those logs and do not receive that information. We do not send any personal information to these services.

Purchases

FlatOut Pro is sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see or store your payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, app-generated identifier and your subscription state so the app can unlock Pro features and restore purchases — it does not receive your name or payment information. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy for details.

Advertising

Version 1 of FlatOut contains no advertising and does no ad tracking. If a future version introduces ads, we will update this policy first, request your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking occurs, and describe exactly what changes.

Children

FlatOut is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app removes all locally stored preferences. You can manage or cancel a subscription anytime in your Apple ID / App Store settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “last updated” date.
