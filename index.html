Spoiler-free

Spoiler protection that actually works.

Most apps "hide" results and then leak them in a notification, a widget, or a stray standings number. FlatOut treats spoilers as a first-class problem — one switch, and the whole app keeps the weekend a secret until you say go.

  • App-wide shield — results, times and standings all stay hidden, not just the headline.
  • Spoiler-safe notifications — reminders that never give the result away.
  • Reveal on your schedule — flip it off the moment you've watched.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is FlatOut official?

No. FlatOut is an independent, unofficial companion made by fans. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Formula 1 or the FIA.

Does the spoiler-free mode really work?

Yes — that's the whole point. One switch hides results, times and standings across the entire app, and reminders are written to never reveal the outcome.

What platforms is it on?

iOS first, with Android to follow. Home-screen widgets are included on iOS.

Where does the data come from?

Session times, standings and results are sourced from established motorsport data providers, refreshed throughout each weekend.
