Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is FlatOut official?

No. FlatOut is an independent, unofficial companion made by fans. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Formula 1, the FIA, Formula One Management, or any team or driver.

Does the spoiler-free mode really work?

Yes — that's the whole point. One switch hides results, times and standings across the entire app, and reminders are written to never reveal the outcome.

What platforms is it on?

On iOS now — download it from the App Store. Android is arriving in October. Home-screen widgets are included on iOS.

Where does the data come from?

Session times, standings and results are sourced from established motorsport data providers, refreshed throughout each weekend.
