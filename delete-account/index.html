Delete your FlatOut account

FlatOut · published by Neo Media Productions Inc.

You can permanently delete your FlatOut account and its data at any time, directly in the app. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open FlatOut and make sure you’re signed in.
  2. Go to your Profile (tap your avatar in the top-left of the standings screen).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account is deleted immediately.

Prefer to email us?

If you can’t access the app, email support@flatoutapp.app from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We’ll confirm and remove it for you.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes the following from our backend:

  • Your account and email address
  • Your profile — username (@handle), display name, and profile photo
  • Your favorites and app preferences
  • Your ratings and reviews

What may be kept, and for how long

Subscription and purchase records that we are required to keep for tax, accounting, or app-store compliance may be retained as required by law, and are not linked to a usable account once yours is deleted. Everything else listed above is deleted right away. Deleting the app from your device also clears any locally cached data. A paid subscription is managed by the app store — cancel it in your Apple ID / Google Play account settings.
